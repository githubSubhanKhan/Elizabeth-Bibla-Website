What the office designs

One garden, four seasons

Flowering dogwood and serviceberry open the year, before the canopy fills in.

Coneflower, bee balm and black-eyed Susan carry color through the heat, and feed pollinators.

Little bluestem turns copper, asters bloom late, and oakleaf hydrangea goes deep red.

Winterberry, red twig dogwood and evergreens keep structure and color in the snow.

How a project runs

  1. 1

    Walk the site

    An on-site consultation to read the light, soil and existing trees, with a review of any survey you have.

  2. 2

    Concept plan

    Layout, circulation and a planting palette, drawn to scale for your property.

  3. 3

    Renderings and revisions

    Color renderings you can react to. The design changes until you're satisfied.

  4. 4

    Plans to build from

    Final drawings ready for the installer you pick, so you can compare bids.
